Discover the new exclusive dating app from Animal Crossing.

ABOUT

This app helps you find villagers of all types.

First up: the SWIPPING.

You can either move to the next villager swiping left or to swipe right to drop a little heart on the one who makes you go "aww" to head out on a date.
Your call.

Roam the seas or stay on your island to discover villagers with the same goal as you: to find love.

Based on your location, Acrinder will find you your perfect match according to the distance of the suitors

Each villager has a distinct personality, so pick your date wisely. The app includes all the important info about each one.

And relax... The zodiac sign is a necessary field (we´re not looking to get hit with a lawsuit or anything o_o).
